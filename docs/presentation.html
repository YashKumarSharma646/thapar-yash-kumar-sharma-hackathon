Core challenge: the NLP risk engine

A 7B LLM's judgement, distilled into a 33M model that runs on CPU

Teacher: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (open weights) labelled 20.8k headlines and tweets with event type, sentiment and severity on a Colab L4 GPU.

Student: bge-small (33M parameters) fine-tuned with three heads, exported to int8 ONNX.

Honest evaluation: the demo window (4,941 docs) was held out of training. Scores are agreement with the teacher, not human labels.

Impact score

Impact is calibrated to what markets actually did

Impact estimates P(material move): a 2-day market-adjusted return of at least 2σ for that stock or region. A logistic model, fitted on {{N_CALIB}} historical signals (2014–2020, demo window excluded), using severity, sentiment, event type, buzz and source.

Impact ≥ 7, the stress-test trigger, means the top 3% of historical signals by predicted move probability.

Every signal shows its drivers in log-odds:

Challenges, limitations and next steps

What we would tell a risk committee

Limitations, stated plainly

  • NLP scores measure agreement with an LLM teacher, not human analysts; a human-labelled test set is the next step.
  • Impact predicts move size modestly (AUC in the mid-0.6s); it is a triage signal, not a trading signal.
  • The portfolio is synthetic; regional CDS widening is assumed where free data does not exist.
  • Pricing is first-order (sensitivities), CET1 covers credit RWA only, losses are pre-tax.
  • Replay of 2018 data, not a live feed.

Next steps

  • Live ingestion (GDELT, RSS) behind the same RawDocument contract.
  • Reverse stress test: find the smallest scenario that breaches a CET1 floor.
  • Rating-migration matrices instead of PD scaling; full revaluation for options.
  • Module A: feed signals into an ESG/risk-tilted index rebalancer.
  • Human-in-the-loop: analysts confirm or reject alerts, and the labels retrain the student.

Everything here is open source and reproducible: docker compose up runs the full demo offline; scripts rebuild every dataset, label, model and calibration from public data.
